About TraceGuard

Why TraceGuard Exists: The Dual-Evidence Challenge in Construction & Fabrication

Construction and fabrication teams require reliable evidence for: Quality inspections, Safety compliance, Client handovers, and Regulatory checks.

The Problem with Fragmented Systems

Current manual documentation chaos

Construction and fabrication businesses currently manage important evidence through:

  • Paper weld records
  • Phone photographs
  • Spreadsheets
  • Manual safety documents
  • Verbal site updates

This creates difficulties during:

• Client Audits • HSE Inspections • Project Handovers • Defect Investigations

The Connected Reality of SME Supervisors

Quality, Safety, and Labour in a Single Capture

TraceGuard combines quality evidence and safety evidence into one unified digital compliance platform. Both areas are connected because SME site supervisors often manage welding quality, safety compliance, and labour allocation on the same project.

Quality Evidence

Digital weld inspection and certification records.

Safety & Resource Evidence

Site safety documentation, workforce allocation and operational tracking.
