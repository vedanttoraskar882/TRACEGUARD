About TraceGuard

Why TraceGuard Exists: The Dual-Evidence Challenge in Construction & Fabrication

Construction and fabrication teams require rigorous, indisputable evidence for four non-negotiable milestones: Quality inspections, Safety compliance, Client handovers, and Regulatory checks.

The Problem with Fragmented Systems

Current manual documentation chaos

Construction and fabrication businesses currently manage critical project evidence through disconnected, error-prone channels:

  • Paper weld records lost or soiled on the shop floor
  • Phone photographs buried in WhatsApp group chats
  • Spreadsheets disconnected from actual site reality
  • Manual safety documents filed weeks after task completion
  • Verbal site updates with zero audit traceability

Severe Friction Points Caused:

• Client Audits • HSE Inspections • Project Handovers • Defect Investigations

The Connected Reality of SME Supervisors

Quality, Safety, and Labour in a Single Capture

In SME fabrication workshops and construction sites, a supervisor is rarely confined to just one role. On any given project, the same supervisor simultaneously manages welding quality, safety compliance, and labour allocation.

Treating quality and safety as separate software tools creates double the administrative burden. TraceGuard unites both domains into one intuitive mobile interface:

Quality Evidence

Digital weld inspection records, WPS/WPQR references, welder qualifications, and client-ready compliance certification under BS EN ISO 3834.

Safety & Resource Evidence

Site safety documentation, digital RAMS sign-offs, plant & equipment inspection verification, and real-time workforce allocation tracking.
